HOW BUDGIT USES DATA TO DRIVE SOCIAL IMPACT IN NIGERIA

Organization: BudgIT Foundation
Country: Nigeria
Focus Area: Data, Technology, Transparency and Accountability

1. Who are they?

BudgIT is a Nigerian civic technology organization founded in 2011. It uses technology, data analysis and citizen engagement to promote transparency and accountability in government spending.

One of its major initiatives is Tracka, a platform that enables citizens to monitor government-funded projects in their communities.

2. What data do they use?

BudgIT uses:

* Government budget allocations and expenditure records.
* Information about government-funded projects and their locations.
* Project implementation and completion records.
* Community feedback, photographs and reports from citizens.
* Information about government agencies and contractors.

Through Tracka, citizens can report the progress or condition of projects in their communities.

3. What changed because of it?

BudgIT’s work has helped citizens participate in monitoring public projects and demanding accountability.

Its Tracka initiative has monitored government projects and supported advocacy for improved public services. One example reported by BudgIT is its advocacy that helped bring electricity to Kawu, a community in Niger State, after 30 years without electricity.

This demonstrates how data can help identify problems, encourage accountability and contribute to improvements in people’s lives.

Conclusion

BudgIT demonstrates that data can be a powerful tool for social impact in Africa. By making government information accessible and empowering citizens to act on it, the organization helps bridge the gap between government promises and actual service delivery.
